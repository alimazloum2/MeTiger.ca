# What the metiger.ca website assistant may say

Everything below is taken from the published metiger.ca pages. Edit this file
when the site changes. The assistant is told to answer only from this text.

## Who MeTiger is
- MeTiger Inc. is a remote-first electrical estimating company based in Canada (620 Nine Mile Drive, Bedford, NS B4A 0H4).
- It serves electrical contractors, general contractors, developers, subcontractors, design-build teams and facility teams in all 10 provinces and 3 territories.
- More than 7 years of experience in the Canadian construction market.
- US projects are handled through the separate site metiger.us.

## What MeTiger does
- Electrical estimating, quantity takeoffs and NECA labour-hour summaries for commercial work, with marked-up plans and scope notes.
- Project types: commercial tenant improvement, retail and restaurant, warehouse and light-industrial, power distribution, lighting and controls, low-voltage / fire alarm / data.
- Also: pricing summaries, bid notes, alternates, addenda support and scope review.
- Electrical scope is organized against the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1), the project specifications and the bid documents.
- You work directly with the estimator building your estimate.

## What MeTiger does not do
- MeTiger provides estimating and bid-support services only. It does not provide sealed engineering, stamped drawings or engineer-of-record services.
- Material pricing and supplier quotes are by the bidder; the takeoff highlights the items that need a supplier quote.
- All work is for review only; the bidder verifies before submitting the bid.

## What an estimate package includes
- Quantity takeoff workbook in Excel (material, devices, conduit, conductors and gear counted by system).
- NECA labour-hour summary with the assumptions stated.
- Colour-coded marked-up PDF drawings so quantities trace back to the drawings.
- Material list by system, inclusions and exclusions, an RFI and assumptions list, items not shown on the drawings, alternates and value-engineering notes, long-lead equipment notes.
- Optional pre-bid review call on request.

## How the work is done
- Quantities are taken off in Bluebeam Revu; material and labour workbooks are built in Excel on a NECA labour-unit basis.
- AI speeds up the count; a person reviews scope, assumptions and exclusions before anything is delivered.

## Fees (CAD; applicable GST/HST is added)
Fixed fee by the size of the electrical bid, confirmed with the quote before any work begins.

| Job size (electrical bid) | Takeoff | Takeoff + NECA labour | Rush (under 48 hr) |
| Under $250k | $400 | $600 | $750 |
| $250k to $750k | $600 | $800 | $1,000 |
| $750k to $1.5M | $800 | $1,100 | $1,375 |
| $1.5M to $3M | $1,400 | $1,900 | $2,375 |
| Over $3M | Quoted hourly after drawing review |

- Rush delivery is offered with the takeoff + NECA labour package.
- Hourly rate: $100, minimum 4 hours. Addenda and revisions are billed hourly.
- Electrical contractors: the first quantity takeoff is free (one per company).
- The full table is on the page /pricing/.

## Timing
- Fixed quote within 1 business day, often the same day, of drawing review.
- Typical estimate turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. The turnaround target is confirmed with the fixed quote before estimating starts.

## How to get a quote
- Use the quote form on the home page or at /contact/#quote. Useful details: drawing set or file-sharing link, project location and scope type, takeoff-only or full estimate, bid date, addenda status.
- Drawings: paste a file-sharing link in the form, or use the Dropbox upload link shown in the form.
- No contracts; per-project quote; NDA on request.
- Email: info@metiger.ca.
- Phone: the number is in the bar at the top of every page. An AI assistant answers the phone and takes project details and the bid date; an estimator follows up by email or phone. Do not write the phone number out; point to the top bar.
