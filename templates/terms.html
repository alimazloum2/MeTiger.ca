MeTiger Legal

Terms

The MeTiger website provides commercial electrical estimating information, planning tools, and inquiry forms for contractors and project teams.

Planning content

Calculator outputs and website content are budgetary planning references only. Project-specific pricing requires drawing review, scope confirmation, and written quote communication.

Quote requests

Submitting a contact or quote request does not create a contract. MeTiger may review submitted details and respond with questions, scope clarification, pricing, or next steps.

Fees

Takeoff and NECA labour fees are published on the Electrical Estimating page in CAD and are confirmed in a written quote before any work begins. Addenda and revisions are billed hourly.

Review only

All estimating deliverables are for the bidder's review. The bidder verifies quantities, assumptions, and exclusions before submitting a bid. Material pricing and supplier quotes are by the bidder.

Website use

Do not submit unlawful content, sensitive personal information unrelated to a project inquiry, or materials you are not authorized to share.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be submitted through the MeTiger contact page.
