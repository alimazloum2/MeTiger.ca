Services

What we do

Electrical Engineering

More than design, we bridge engineering intent with construction execution. MeTiger provides coordinated, code-compliant electrical solutions that enhance safety, energy efficiency, and reliability across every project stage.

Project Management

Working behind the scenes to keep every detail aligned before it becomes a problem. MeTiger reviews scopes, tracks RFIs and submittals, and guides subcontract negotiations to ensure the work flows with fewer change orders and no surprises later. We prepare and review cost forecasts, progress claims, and subcontract invoices with accuracy and fairness, maintaining control of budgets and cash flow. Every stage is managed quietly but effectively, connecting design intent, cost, and field execution so projects stay steady from start to finish.

Construction Documentation

Supporting both tender and construction phases, preparing RFQs, tender front-end packages, and addenda before award, then managing CDs, CCNs, and CCOs after award to keep scope, cost, and drawings fully aligned.

Tender Review & Cost Estimating

Supporting both builders and clients with a fresh, experienced eye. We review drawings, verify quantities, and help prepare organized trade packages, bringing clarity, balance, and efficiency to every tender.

Projects

Selected work

Institutional Building – West Bedford School

Provided project management and coordination support for the new West Bedford School, a 253,000 sq ft pre-primary to grade 12 facility delivered under a collaborative design-build model. MeTiger contributed behind the scenes to electrical coordination, design reviews, and construction management support focused on energy efficiency and timely delivery.

Healthcare Facility Renovation – QEII Hospital Integration Work

Supported electrical integration and renovation work at the QEII Health Sciences Centre, including UPS and ATS upgrades. Focused on phased execution, minimizing disruption to ongoing hospital operations, and ensuring compliance with healthcare safety and electrical standards.

Energy & Infrastructure Project – Nova Scotia Power Solar Initiative

Assisted with project coordination, engineering input, and construction management for Nova Scotia Power's solar program and related substation upgrades. Work emphasized reliability, schedule alignment, and seamless integration with existing infrastructure.
