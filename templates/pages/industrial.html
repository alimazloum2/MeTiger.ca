Gear & long-lead

Long-lead equipment, flagged early

On industrial work, the schedule risk lives in the gear. Major distribution equipment can carry long procurement windows, and a missed lead time hurts more than a missed device count.

Your package includes a gear and long-lead note that isolates switchgear, MCCs, transformers, generators, large VFDs, and bus duct so your team can request budget pricing and lead times before bid day. MeTiger quantifies and organises the gear from the documents; final vendor pricing, submittals, and delivery commitments remain yours to confirm with your suppliers and the engineer of record.
Approach

CEC-aware industrial estimating

Our takeoff and scope review are informed by CSA C22.1 / the Canadian Electrical Code, provincial amendments and practices, project specifications, utility requirements, and the bid documents. We estimate what the documents call for, apply project-specific labour assumptions and Canadian market pricing assumptions, and flag where the drawings, schedules, and specs do not agree.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ), and applicable provincial or territorial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support — not stamped drawings, sealed professional engineering, or code-compliance certification — and works alongside the engineer of record where required.
