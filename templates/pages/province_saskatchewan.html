Local context

Electrical estimating built for Saskatchewan projects

Saskatchewan electrical work is estimated against the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1) as adopted in the province, with electrical inspection and permitting administered by the Technical Safety Authority of Saskatchewan (TSASK) and the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ). Service, metering, and connection details are coordinated with the serving utility — SaskPower — across the province.

We estimate what your bid documents call for and reconcile it against those local realities: SaskPower service and metering arrangements, provincial code amendments, and the practices common on prairie commercial, agri-industrial, and institutional work. When the drawings and specifications don't agree, we flag it in writing rather than burying the assumption in the number. Remote delivery means we support your Saskatoon, Regina, or rural Saskatchewan bid on the same footing — you send the set, we return a package your team can defend line by line.

Code & compliance

Saskatchewan code & compliance

Our estimating and scope review are informed by the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1) as adopted in Saskatchewan, along with provincial amendments, the project specifications, addenda, and SaskPower service requirements. This is CEC-aware estimating support: we quantify and coordinate what the documents require, and we surface where they conflict.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the Technical Safety Authority of Saskatchewan (TSASK) and the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ), and applicable provincial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support — not stamped drawings, sealed or professional engineering, or code-compliance certification — and works alongside the engineer of record where required.
