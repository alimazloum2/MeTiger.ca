FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide electrical estimating across Canada?

Yes. MeTiger provides electrical estimating and takeoff support remotely for contractors and general contractors in every province and territory. We work from your PDF or DWG set and deliver a reviewable estimate package on an agreed window.

Do you use the Canadian Electrical Code (CEC)?

Our estimating and scope review are informed by the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1), project specifications, local utility requirements, and the bid documents. Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the authority having jurisdiction, and applicable provincial/territorial requirements.

Can you estimate from PDF drawings?

Yes. PDF bid sets are the most common input. Send the drawings, specifications, and any addenda, and we confirm scope before starting.

Can you work from DWG / CAD files?

Yes. We work from DWG/CAD backgrounds as well as PDFs. Let us know what format you have and we will confirm what we need.

What do I receive with the estimate?

A reviewable package: a quantity takeoff workbook, colour-coded marked-up drawings, a material list by system, a NECA labour-hour summary with the labour-unit basis and assumptions, inclusions and exclusions, an RFI / scope-gap log, addenda review, and a bid summary.

Do you provide stamped engineering?

No. MeTiger provides estimating and bid-support services — not sealed engineering, stamped drawings, or engineer-of-record services.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. An NDA is available on request — note it on the form and we will send one before you share drawings.

How is pricing structured?

A fixed estimating quote per project, confirmed after we review the drawing set, scope, and bid schedule — before any work begins. No subscription and no long-term contract. Takeoffs run $400 to $1,400 CAD and takeoff plus NECA labour $600 to $1,900 CAD by bid size, plus applicable GST/HST; bids over $3M are quoted hourly after drawing review. Electrical contractors: your first quantity takeoff is free (one per company).

How fast is turnaround?

Typical 24–48 hour target, confirmed per project after drawing review. Larger or incomplete sets may take longer.

How do I request a quote?

Send your bid set (or a plan link) and the bid due date through the quote form. We confirm scope, fee, and delivery window before any work begins.
