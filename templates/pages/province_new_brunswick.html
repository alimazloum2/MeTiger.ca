Local context

Electrical estimating built for New Brunswick projects

New Brunswick electrical work follows the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1) as adopted in the province, with inspection and permitting handled through the New Brunswick electrical inspection authority and the local authority having jurisdiction. We estimate to what the tender documents call for and keep that provincial context in view while we count.

Most of the province is served by NB Power, and service entrance, metering, and utility-coordination details can shape the electrical scope on any given job. We account for the servicing shown on your drawings, note where the utility interface is left unclear, and flag it so the question can be settled before bid day rather than after award. New Brunswick is also an officially bilingual market, so we can work from tender documents issued in English or French.

Code & compliance

New Brunswick code & compliance

Our estimating and scope review are informed by the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1) as adopted in New Brunswick, alongside the project specifications, drawings, addenda, and local utility requirements. We estimate what the documents describe and note where they conflict or leave a gap, so nothing is priced on a silent assumption.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the New Brunswick authority having jurisdiction, and applicable provincial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support only. We do not produce stamped drawings, sealed or professional engineering, or code-compliance certification, and we work alongside the engineer of record where required.
