Local context

Electrical estimating built for Alberta projects

Alberta electrical work is administered under the province's Safety Codes Act, largely through accredited municipalities and agencies overseen by the Safety Codes Council, with permitting handled by the provincial or municipal authority having jurisdiction for your project. We keep that framework in mind while we price the scope.

Utility coordination matters on Alberta bids, and service arrangements differ by territory — ENMAX in Calgary, EPCOR in Edmonton, and FortisAlberta or ATCO Electric across much of the rest of the province. We note the service entrance, metering, and utility-coordination assumptions in the estimate so the connection scope is visible before bid day rather than after award.

Code & compliance

Alberta code & compliance

Our estimating and scope review are informed by the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1) as adopted in Alberta, alongside project specifications, local utility requirements, and the bid documents. We estimate what the documents call for and log where they conflict or fall silent so you can raise it as an RFI.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the Alberta authority having jurisdiction, and applicable provincial requirements. MeTiger provides CEC-aware estimating and coordination support — not stamped drawings, sealed engineering, or code-compliance certification — and works alongside the engineer of record where required.
