Local context

Electrical estimating built for Quebec projects

Quebec runs its own electrical regime, and our estimating support reflects it. Electrical construction in the province is overseen by the Régie du bâtiment du Québec (RBQ), and the electrical trade is governed by the Corporation des maîtres électriciens du Québec (CMEQ). The governing document is the Quebec Construction Code, Chapter V – Electricity, based on CSA C22.10 — the provincial standard derived from the Canadian Electrical Code.

Distribution across most of the province is served by Hydro-Québec, so utility service, metering, and coordination notes are read with local practice in mind. Because Quebec is a French-first market, we estimate directly from tender drawings and specifications issued in French or in English, and flag anything that needs a clarification rather than guessing at intent.

We provide estimating and coordination support informed by CSA C22.10 and the Quebec Construction Code, Chapter V. We do not act as the RBQ-licensed contractor, the engineer of record, or the authority having jurisdiction.

Code & compliance

Quebec code & compliance

Our takeoffs and scope review are informed by the Quebec Construction Code, Chapter V – Electricity, which is based on CSA C22.10 — the provincial standard derived from the Canadian Electrical Code — together with the project specifications, Hydro-Québec service requirements, and the tender documents. We estimate what the drawings call for and flag where they conflict or leave gaps.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the Quebec authority having jurisdiction, and applicable provincial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support only — not stamped drawings, sealed engineering, or code-compliance certification — and works alongside the engineer of record where required.
