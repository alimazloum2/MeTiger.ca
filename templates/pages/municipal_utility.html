Project types

Municipal & utility projects we estimate

Public infrastructure electrical carries its own rhythm — process-driven layouts, controls tie-ins, and tender rules. These are the projects we quantify most.

Pump & lift stations

Sanitary and stormwater lift stations, booster and pumping stations with wet-well instrumentation, controls, and standby power.

Water & wastewater treatment

Roads & site lighting

Tenders & addenda

Built for public tenders and addenda-heavy bids

Municipal and utility work closes on public tender documents, and those documents move — addenda revise pump schedules, MCC layouts, controls narratives, and site work right up to closing. We read the front-end package, not just the drawings, and reconcile every addendum against the base set so a late change does not walk out of your number.

  • Front-end and specification review alongside the drawings
  • Addenda logged and reconciled against the base tender set
  • Electrical cross-checked against process, mechanical, and instrumentation scope
  • Documented assumptions and exclusions — not silent guesses
Send every addendum as it is issued. Municipal closings often carry several rounds, and reconciling them against the base set is where missed scope and margin leaks usually hide.
Code & approach

CEC-aware estimating support

Our takeoff and scope review are informed by CSA C22.1 / the Canadian Electrical Code, provincial amendments and practices, the project specifications, and local utility requirements. We estimate what the tender documents call for, in the classified and process areas the engineer defines, and flag where the documents do not agree.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ), and applicable provincial or territorial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support — not stamped drawings, sealed professional engineering, or code-compliance certification — and works alongside the engineer of record where required.
