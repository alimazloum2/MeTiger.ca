Local context

Electrical estimating built for Ontario projects

Ontario electrical work is inspected by the Electrical Safety Authority (ESA), and installations follow the Ontario Electrical Safety Code (OESC) — the Canadian Electrical Code, CSA C22.1, adopted with Ontario amendments. We estimate to what the tender documents call for and keep that provincial context in view while we count.

Projects across the province also touch different local distribution utilities, and service, metering, and utility-coordination notes can differ between them. We account for the servicing details shown on your drawings whether the work sits in Hydro One, Toronto Hydro, Alectra Utilities, Hydro Ottawa, or Elexicon Energy territory, and we flag where the documents leave the utility interface unclear so it can be resolved before bid day.

Code & compliance

Ontario code & compliance

Our estimating and scope review are informed by the Ontario Electrical Safety Code (OESC) — the Canadian Electrical Code, CSA C22.1, adopted with Ontario amendments — alongside the project specifications, drawings, addenda, and local utility requirements. We estimate what the documents describe and note where they conflict or leave a gap, so nothing is priced on a silent assumption.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the Ontario authority having jurisdiction — including the Electrical Safety Authority (ESA) — and applicable provincial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support only. We do not produce stamped drawings, sealed or professional engineering, or code-compliance certification, and we work alongside the engineer of record where required.
