Everything you need to plug our quantities into your own pricing — and to trace every number back to the sheet it came from.

  • Colour-coded marked-up drawings for verification

A takeoff is delivered as quantities and counts — not priced unless you ask for pricing. That keeps your labour rates and material margins entirely yours. Need dollars attached? We can quote a full estimate instead.
Takeoff vs full estimate

Where a takeoff stops and an estimate begins

Both start from the same careful count. The difference is how far we take it — and how much of the pricing stays in your hands.

Quantity takeoff

Quantities and counts by device, fixture, conduit, conductor, and gear, with colour-coded marked-up drawings and documented assumptions. You apply labour units and material pricing. Best when you own your own rates.

Full electrical estimate

Everything in the takeoff, plus a labour-hour summary built on project-specific labour assumptions, material pricing, and a bid summary you can hand to a client. Best when you want a ready-to-carry number.

Not sure which you need? Send the set and we'll recommend the right one when we quote. Prefer a priced number end to end? See our Canadian electrical estimating services.

Approach

Counted carefully, documented honestly

Our counts and scope notes are informed by CSA C22.1 / the Canadian Electrical Code, provincial practices, project specifications, and the bid documents. We quantify what the drawings call for and flag where they don't agree — but a takeoff is a measurement of the documents, not a compliance sign-off.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ), and applicable provincial/territorial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support — not stamped drawings, sealed engineering, or code-compliance certification — and works alongside the engineer of record where required.

Pricing and quote process

Every takeoff is quoted after drawing review, based on sheet count, scope complexity, project type, deadline, and whether you want quantities only or full pricing. Send the set and the deadline; MeTiger confirms scope, fee, and delivery window before starting — no surprises, no cheap-race pricing.
