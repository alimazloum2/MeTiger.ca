How it works

How our quotes work

There is no public rate sheet, and that is deliberate. Electrical estimating cost depends on what the drawings actually contain, so we price each bid on its merits — a fair fee for real scope, put in writing before we start, with nothing added quietly later.

Engagement types

Ways to work with us

Pick the level of support that fits the bid. Each engagement is scoped and priced the same way — after we review your drawings.

Takeoff-only support

A clean quantity takeoff by system when your team carries the pricing. You get counts and marked-up drawings and apply your own labour units and market pricing assumptions.

Full bid-ready estimate

The complete package: takeoff, material list, labour-hour summary, colour-coded marked-up drawings, assumptions, exclusions, and a bid summary you can submit with confidence.

Bid review & second set of eyes

Already have a number? We review your takeoff and scope for gaps, missed systems, and soft assumptions before you commit it to a tender.

Monthly / ongoing support

Recurring estimating capacity for busy shops — a predictable arrangement for contractors bidding steadily, scoped around your typical volume.

Rush support when available

Tight deadline? When our schedule allows, we take on rush bid sets. Rush availability and any adjustment are confirmed with your quote, never assumed.

What a quote does not include

{{ checks([ 'Stamped or sealed professional engineering', 'Permit drawings or permit applications', 'Authority having jurisdiction (AHJ) approval or code certification', 'Material procurement, purchasing, or supplier negotiation', 'On-site supervision or installation labour' ]) }}
MeTiger provides electrical estimating and coordination support — not stamped drawings, sealed engineering, or code-compliance certification. Our takeoffs are informed by CSA C22.1 / the Canadian Electrical Code and the bid documents; final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ), and applicable provincial/territorial requirements. We work alongside the engineer of record where required.
