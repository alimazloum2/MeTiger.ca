Provincial context

Electrical estimating built for Nova Scotia projects

Nova Scotia electrical work is governed by the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1) as adopted in the province, with electrical inspection and permitting administered provincially through the Nova Scotia authority having jurisdiction. We estimate what the tender documents call for and reconcile them against those requirements.

On the utility side, most projects connect to the Nova Scotia Power distribution network, and service coordination, metering, and utility interface notes are part of how we frame the electrical scope. We flag where the drawings, specifications, and utility requirements need to line up so your service and distribution numbers hold together before you commit to a price.

Estimating and coordination support only. Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the Nova Scotia authority having jurisdiction, and applicable provincial requirements — MeTiger works alongside the engineer of record where required and does not provide stamped or sealed engineering.

Code & compliance

Nova Scotia code & compliance

Our estimating and scope review are informed by CSA C22.1 / the Canadian Electrical Code as adopted in Nova Scotia, together with project specifications, Nova Scotia Power utility requirements, and the bid documents. We estimate what the documents call for and flag where they don’t agree — so pricing rests on a defensible reading of scope, not guesswork.

MeTiger provides CEC-aware estimating and coordination support — not stamped drawings, sealed or professional engineering, permit-ready engineering, or code-compliance certification. Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the Nova Scotia authority having jurisdiction, and applicable provincial requirements. We work alongside the engineer of record where required.
