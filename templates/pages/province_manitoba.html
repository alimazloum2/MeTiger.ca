Local context

Electrical estimating built for Manitoba projects

Manitoba electrical work is estimated against the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1) as adopted in the province, with electrical inspection and permitting handled by the provincial electrical authority and the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ). Service, metering, and connection details are coordinated with the serving utility — Manitoba Hydro — across the province.

We estimate what your bid documents call for and reconcile it against those local realities: Manitoba Hydro service and metering arrangements, provincial code amendments, and the practices common on prairie commercial, warehouse, and institutional work. When the drawings and specifications don't agree, we flag it in writing rather than burying the assumption in the number. Remote delivery means we support your Winnipeg or rural Manitoba bid on the same footing — you send the set, we return a package your team can check line by line.

Code & compliance

Manitoba code & compliance

Our estimating and scope review are informed by the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1) as adopted in Manitoba, along with provincial amendments, the project specifications, addenda, and Manitoba Hydro service requirements. This is CEC-aware estimating support: we quantify and coordinate what the documents require, and we surface where they conflict.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the Manitoba authority having jurisdiction (AHJ), and applicable provincial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support — not stamped drawings, sealed or professional engineering, or code-compliance certification — and works alongside the engineer of record where required.
