Phasing & programs

Live-store phasing and multi-site consistency

Retail rarely happens on an empty site. Remodels and refreshes often run around live store operations, and program work has to stay consistent across dozens of locations. We build both realities into the estimate so the labour impact is visible before award, not discovered on site.

  • Night, after-hours, and off-hours work reflected where the documents require it
  • Temporary power and phased-cutover assumptions logged clearly
  • Demolition and existing-condition allowances called out for remodels
  • A repeatable takeoff basis so rollout pricing stays comparable location to location
  • Per-site adjustments for base-building conditions, regional labour, and local requirements
Working around a live store changes labour productivity and sequencing. We document our phasing and temporary-power assumptions so your team can price the impact rather than absorb it after the fact.
Approach

CEC-aware retail estimating

Our retail estimating support is informed by CSA C22.1 / the Canadian Electrical Code, provincial amendments and practices, tenant design criteria, landlord standards, project specifications, and local utility requirements. We quantify what the documents call for and flag where they do not agree.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ), and applicable provincial or territorial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support — not stamped drawings, sealed engineering, or code-compliance certification — and works alongside the engineer of record where required.
