Local context

Electrical estimating built for British Columbia projects

British Columbia electrical work runs on the Canadian Electrical Code as adopted in the province with British Columbia amendments. Permitting and inspection are handled by Technical Safety BC across most of the province, while some municipalities inspect their own electrical work — so the authority having jurisdiction depends on where the project sits.

We read the same drawings your team does, and we account for the utility context that shapes British Columbia scope. Service coordination often runs through BC Hydro across most of the province and FortisBC in parts of the south and the Kootenays, and we flag service, metering, and utility-interface items so nothing is assumed. From Vancouver, Surrey, Burnaby, and Richmond through Victoria, Kelowna, Kamloops, and Nanaimo, our takeoffs stay grounded in how projects are actually built and inspected here.

Code & compliance

British Columbia code & compliance

Our estimating and scope review are informed by the Canadian Electrical Code (CSA C22.1) as adopted in British Columbia with the province's amendments, together with the project specifications, utility requirements, and the bid documents. We estimate what the documents call for and flag where they don't agree.

Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the British Columbia authority having jurisdiction — typically Technical Safety BC or a municipality that inspects its own electrical work — and applicable provincial requirements. MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support only — not stamped drawings, sealed engineering, or code-compliance certification — and works alongside the engineer of record where required.
