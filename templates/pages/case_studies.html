Group 1

Representative prior estimating experience

The following are anonymized, generic descriptions of prior estimating work, grouped by project type. They are shown to convey the scope, systems, and coordination involved — no owners, brands, stores, or locations are identified.

Large-format retail interior remodel

Takeoff and labour summary for a big-box interior renovation: LED lighting upgrades and controls, refrigeration and case power, back-of-house branch circuits, and receptacle and equipment connections. Scope was organized so the count could be phased and priced around live store operations, with clear inclusions, exclusions, and after-hours assumptions documented for the bid.

Restaurant tenant improvement build-out

Estimate for a restaurant fit-out inside a leased shell: kitchen equipment power, exhaust hood interlocks and controls coordination, dining-room lighting and controls, and front-of-house receptacles. The package flagged tenant-versus-landlord scope splits, base-building tie-in points, and equipment with unclear power sources so the contractor could resolve them before pricing.

Municipal wastewater lift-station package

Electrical takeoff for municipal wastewater lift-station work: pump control panels, motor feeders and disconnects, SCADA tie-in provisions, and standby generator and automatic transfer switch (ATS) provisions. Utility service coordination points, grounding, and site conduit were quantified and noted, with assumptions logged where the documents were incomplete or in conflict.

Representative of scope and project type; shown to illustrate capability.

How to read these

Scope, not spin

Case studies are useful for understanding project types and coordination challenges — but the best way to judge our work is the deliverable itself. Here is what every MeTiger estimate is built to show, on any of the projects above:

  • Quantity takeoff you can check line by line
  • Colour-coded marked-up drawings
  • Material list organized by system
  • Labour-hour summary with the labour-unit basis
  • Documented inclusions and exclusions
  • RFI and scope-gap log for conflicts and missing items
  • Addenda reconciled against the base set
  • Clear assumptions instead of silent guesses
MeTiger provides electrical estimating and coordination support informed by CSA C22.1 / the Canadian Electrical Code, provincial practices, and the bid documents — not stamped drawings, sealed professional engineering, or code-compliance certification. Final compliance remains subject to the project engineer, the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ), and applicable provincial/territorial requirements, and we work alongside the engineer of record where required.
