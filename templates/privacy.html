MeTiger Legal

Privacy Policy

MeTiger collects information that visitors choose to submit through contact and quote forms, including names, work email addresses, company details, project scope, due dates, messages, and drawing or file links.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to respond to inquiries, evaluate estimating requests, coordinate project communication, improve website content, and maintain business records.

Phone calls

Calls to MeTiger's phone line are answered by an AI assistant that takes the caller's name, contact details, project information, and bid date. Calls may be recorded or transcribed so an estimator can follow up accurately. These details are handled the same way as a quote-form submission, and an estimator follows up by email or phone.

Project information

Bid documents, drawings, specifications, and project notes are treated as confidential business information and are used for estimating-related communication and review.

Files shared by link or uploaded through our Dropbox upload page are stored in MeTiger's Dropbox and treated as confidential. Drawings and bid documents are kept for up to 12 months after the estimate is delivered — and deleted earlier at the client's request — so addenda, revisions, and follow-up bids are easy to support.

Website data

The site may collect basic technical information such as pages visited, browser type, and device information through hosting, analytics, or security tools.

Website chat assistant

The chat on this website is answered by an AI assistant. Messages you type are processed by an AI service and stored for up to 90 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the MeTiger contact page.
