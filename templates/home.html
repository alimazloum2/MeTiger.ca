Services overview

Commercial electrical estimating support.

Use MeTiger for takeoffs, pricing summaries, bid notes, alternates, addenda support, and scope review on commercial tenant improvement, retail, restaurant, warehouse, power distribution, lighting, controls, and low-voltage scopes.

Retail & Restaurant

Storefront and kitchen scopes with equipment-heavy loads, dedicated circuits, GFCI assumptions, signage, and tenant coordination notes.
