The problem

Stop bidding from incomplete electrical scope.

Electrical costs are often missed between drawings, specifications, addenda, schedules, and other trades’ equipment requirements. MeTiger reviews the bid set, organizes the electrical scope, flags missing information, and delivers a reviewable estimate package your team can price, check, and submit with more confidence.

Services

Electrical estimating support, end to end

Canadian Commercial Electrical Estimating

Tenant improvements, retail, offices, and multi-family — takeoffs and labour summaries built for review.

Industrial & Utility Electrical Estimating

Warehouses, plants, pump stations, and site electrical, with gear and long-lead notes where applicable.

Electrical Takeoffs

Standalone quantity takeoffs by system with colour-coded marked-up drawings you can check line by line.

Electrical Bid Review

An independent second set of eyes on your drawings, quantities, and exclusions before you submit.

Construction Documentation Support

RFQ, front-end, and addenda review before award; change documentation after award to keep scope aligned.

Electrical Design Coordination Support

Coordination that bridges design intent and field execution, working alongside the engineer of record where required.
MeTiger provides estimating and coordination support — not sealed engineering, stamped drawings, or authority-having-jurisdiction approval.
