Privacy Policy

MeTiger Inc. — Updated July 4, 2026

MeTiger Inc. (“MeTiger,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an electrical estimating and construction-support consultancy. Our registered office is in Bedford, Nova Scotia, Canada, and we provide estimating support to clients across Canada. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information submitted through metiger.ca.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily submit through our contact and quote-request forms, which may include:

  • Name and company/organization
  • Work email address and phone number
  • Project location/province, project type, and service needed
  • Bid due date, scope notes, and any plan link you choose to share
  • Message content

How Information Is Used

  • Form submissions are transmitted to our server and emailed directly to our business inbox (info@metiger.ca).
  • We use the information solely to respond to your request, prepare quotes, and communicate with you about your project.
  • We do not store form submissions in a website database, and we do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Plan Links & Documents

Our quote forms accept a “plan link” (for example a Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Procore, or BuildingConnected link) rather than direct file uploads. Please do not share confidential tender documents unless you are authorized to do so. An NDA is available on request before you send project files.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first, cookieless analytics tool that reports aggregate traffic (such as page views and referrers). It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not track you across other websites. We do not use advertising pixels, marketing-automation tracking, or user accounts.

Data Retention

We retain email communications only as long as reasonably necessary for business and record-keeping purposes.

Cross-Border Processing

MeTiger is registered in Canada and uses cloud infrastructure and email services that may process or store data in Canada or the United States. By submitting a form, you acknowledge that your message may be processed in either country. If you require Canadian-only data handling for sensitive documents, contact us before sharing files.

Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect information transmitted through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may request access to or deletion of personal information you have submitted by emailing info@metiger.ca.
